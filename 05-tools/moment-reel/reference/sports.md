# Sports: the facts to get and the real sizes

Real sizes make the scene believable, and the facts list is what step 1 must find before anything is written. All sizes in metres.

## Football
- **Facts to get:** date, venue, competition and round, the score before and after, the minute, who passed and who scored (which foot or body part), where it was struck from, where it went in, the keeper, both teams' kits that day (shirt, shorts, socks) and the keepers' kits, any short famous quote with who said it and when.
- **Sizes:** pitch about 105 x 68 (old grounds vary: use the source's size), goal 7.32 x 2.44, penalty area 16.5 deep x 40.32 wide, six-yard box 5.5 x 18.32, penalty spot 11 out, the D and centre circle radius 9.15.
- **Heights:** use the sources' heights only as ratios between players; when sources disagree, show "taller" without a number.

## Basketball
- **Facts to get:** date, arena, the game (and the series score for a playoff game), the score before and after, the clock to the tenth for a buzzer beater, who shot and from where, who defended, both teams' uniforms that night.
- **Sizes:** NBA court 28.65 x 15.24 (FIBA 28 x 15), rim 3.05 high, its centre 1.6 in from the baseline, backboard 1.83 x 1.07, three-point line 7.24 (FIBA 6.75), key 4.88 wide (FIBA 4.9) and 5.79 deep.

## Boxing
- **Facts to get:** date, venue, the round and the time in it, the punch (which hand, what kind), the result as officially recorded, both fighters' trunk colours.
- **Sizes:** ring 4.9-7.3 inside the ropes (sources give feet: 16-24 ft), four ropes at about 0.45, 0.8, 1.15 and 1.45 above the canvas, the platform about 1 m up.

## Anything else
Tennis, cricket, athletics and the rest: take the court or field size from the sport's rulebook, list it in `sources.md`, and get the same depth of facts: when, where, who, the act itself, the score or result, and what each side wore.
