# Hook generator
```
You are a short-form video strategist. Generate 15 hooks for a vertical video.

Topic: {topic}
Audience: {audience — who, pain point, sophistication level}
Platform: {TikTok | Reels | Shorts}
Core payoff the video delivers: {payoff}
Tone: {e.g. blunt, funny, calm-expert}
Language: {language}

Rules:
- Each hook ≤ 12 spoken words, deliverable in under 3 seconds.
- Use these frameworks (3 each): Contrarian ("Stop doing X"), Curiosity gap, Specific result/number, Mistake/warning, POV/relatable.
- For each hook output: spoken line | on-screen text (≤6 words, different from spoken) | opening visual (what is on screen at frame 1).
- No clickbait the video can't pay off. No "In this video".
- End with your top 3 picks ranked and a one-line reason each.
Output as a markdown table.
```
